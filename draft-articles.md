## Article II Purposes

The corporation is organized exclusively for charitable and educational purposes within the meaning of Section 501(c)(3) of the Internal Revenue Code, or the corresponding provision of any future federal tax law.

Its specific purpose is to preserve and advance the tradition of shape-note singing and educate the public about its music, history, and practice through participatory singings, workshops, instruction for adults and children, and related public educational programs in Kalamazoo and the surrounding region.

## Article VI Limitations

The corporation's earnings and assets shall be used to carry out its exempt purposes. They shall not inure to the benefit of any director, officer, or other private person. The corporation may pay reasonable compensation for services actually rendered and reimburse reasonable expenses incurred in carrying out its purposes.

The corporation shall not participate or intervene in any campaign supporting or opposing a candidate for public office, including through the publication or distribution of statements. No substantial part of its activities shall consist of propaganda or efforts to influence legislation.

No power granted to the corporation shall authorize activity inconsistent with exemption under Section 501(c)(3) of the Internal Revenue Code or the deductibility of contributions under Section 170(c)(2), or their corresponding provisions in future federal tax law.

## Article VII Dissolution

Upon dissolution, after paying or making provision for all lawful liabilities, the corporation shall distribute its remaining assets to the Sacred Harp Musical Heritage Association, EIN 63-1216235, for use exclusively for purposes within the meaning of Section 501(c)(3) of the Internal Revenue Code, provided that the Association exists and qualifies as an organization exempt under Section 501(c)(3) both at the time of dissolution and at the time of distribution. All distributions shall comply with applicable law and any legally enforceable restrictions on the assets.

If the Sacred Harp Musical Heritage Association does not exist, does not so qualify, or is unable or unwilling to accept all or part of the assets, the assets it cannot receive shall be distributed to one or more organizations selected by the board that then qualify as exempt under Section 501(c)(3), exclusively for exempt purposes and preferably for the preservation and teaching of shape-note singing. References to Section 501(c)(3) include the corresponding provision of any future federal tax law. No remaining assets shall be distributed to directors, officers, or participants for their private benefit.
