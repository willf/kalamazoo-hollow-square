## Proposed conflict-of-interest policy

The purpose of this policy is to protect the corporation when it considers a transaction that could benefit a director, officer, family member, or associated business personally.

1. Each director and officer shall disclose financial interests and other material personal interests that may affect a corporate decision. Disclosure is required annually and whenever a relevant matter arises. Interests include compensation, ownership, and arrangements involving close family or a business the person controls.
2. The person shall disclose the relevant facts before deliberation. Disinterested directors shall determine whether a conflict exists. The interested person may answer factual questions but shall leave deliberation and voting and shall not try to influence the decision outside the meeting.
3. Before approval, disinterested directors shall consider reasonable alternatives and available information about fair price and terms. They may approve only a transaction they determine serves the corporation's interests and purposes, is fair and reasonable, and complies with applicable law and the bylaws' voting requirements. If there are insufficient disinterested directors for lawful approval, defer the transaction and obtain an appropriate lawful approval process.
4. Minutes shall identify the disclosed interest, the persons present during deliberation and voting, alternatives considered, the basis for approval, and the actual vote.
5. A person shall not approve their own compensation. Any paid service arrangement with a director or officer requires separate disinterested approval under this policy; board service itself is unpaid under the bylaws.
6. If a possible undisclosed conflict comes to the board's attention, it shall give the person an opportunity to explain and take appropriate corrective action based on the facts.
7. Each director and officer shall acknowledge this policy annually. The secretary shall keep the disclosures with the corporate records.

## Authority for initial organization

Michigan's [MCL 450.2223](https://legislature.mi.gov/Laws/MCL?objectName=mcl-450-2223) authorizes incorporator selection of the board and board adoption of bylaws and election of officers. Financial controls and the conflict policy above are proposed internal rules for this organization.
