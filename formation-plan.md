## Proposed mission

Kalamazoo Hollow Square preserves and advances shape-note singing through public participatory singings, instruction for adults and children, and community education about the tradition in Kalamazoo and the surrounding region.

Public access and the mission wording are proposed commitments for the board to confirm.

1. Complete the open fields in the draft articles and approve the mission and proposed bylaws. The draft bylaws propose one-year director terms, at least two board meetings annually, and a calendar fiscal year.
2. File the articles with Michigan LARA using [Form 502](https://www.michigan.gov/lara/-/media/Project/Websites/lara/cscl/NonImages_new/Corps/forms/corporation/502-0725.pdf) or the [online filing portal](https://www.michigan.gov/corpfileonline). Include the federal tax provisions; the state's basic form alone does not supply them. The ordinary state formation fee is $20. 3. After incorporation, complete the incorporator's appointment of the initial directors and hold the organizational meeting. Adopt bylaws and the conflict policy, elect officers, and authorize banking and the exemption application. The initial meeting should be called with at least three days' notice by mail to each director under [MCL 450.2223](https://legislature.mi.gov/Laws/MCL?objectName=mcl-450-2223).
4. Obtain a free [EIN from the IRS](https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/get-an-employer-identification-number), using the actual incorporation date and the legal name accepted by Michigan. William is the proposed responsible party as principal officer. Enter his taxpayer identification number directly in the IRS process, not in these project documents.
5. Open an account in the corporation's name using its EIN, filed articles, adopted resolutions, and the bank's required identification. Confirm the bank's specific requirements. Transfer only the documented group balance from the personal account, preserving donor restrictions. Keep a reconciliation of the old group balance, evidence of the transfer, and the new account's deposit record. Record this as a transfer of existing group funds rather than automatically treating it as a new personal donation by the account holder.
6. Complete every item of the [Form 1023-EZ eligibility worksheet](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i1023ez). The $2,000 budget suggests the financial limits may be met, but confirm actual and projected gross receipts and total assets. Gross receipts are amounts received before expenses. Review the school exclusion against the actual instructional program; calling a workshop a singing school does not by itself settle the classification. Select public-charity classification only after reviewing the expected sources of support.
7. If eligible, prepare Form 1023-EZ for review and authorized signature. Its current fee is $275; the full Form 1023 fee is $600. [IRS fees](https://www.irs.gov/charities-non-profits/form-1023-and-1023-ez-amount-of-user-fee). Although qualifying organizations normally receiving no more than $5,000 may be exempt without applying, the planned route here is to obtain an IRS determination letter. [IRS instructions](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i1023ez)
8. Resolve Michigan Attorney General requirements. At this scale, unpaid fundraising and other required conditions may permit a solicitation exemption, but the separate charitable-trust rules also require review. Use CTS-03 to request confirmation of applicable exemptions; do not assume a small budget resolves both regimes. [Solicitation requirements](https://www.michigan.gov/consumerprotection/charities/charitable-organizations), [charitable-trust requirements](https://www.michigan.gov/consumerprotection/charities/trusts-and-estates)

## Proposed financial controls

Becca Harrington is the proposed treasurer and will oversee financial records, receipts, payments, and financial reports under the bylaws. William remains the proposed president. A document requiring execution by two officers needs two different people. [MCL 450.2531](https://legislature.mi.gov/Laws/MCL?objectName=mcl-450-2531)

Proposed controls: a director other than the person principally handling funds reviews each monthly bank statement with activity; another disinterested director approves each person's reimbursements; spending outside the approved budget requires board authorization. The draft resolutions leave the independent reviewer to be selected. These are internal choices for adoption, not claims about statutory requirements.

### Bylaws review and adoption

The proposed bylaws provide for five directors, one-year terms, a calendar fiscal year, and at least two board meetings per year. They take effect upon board adoption after incorporation. Record officer names and elections in the organizational minutes rather than in the continuing bylaws. Complete the adoption date and secretary's certification when adopted.

Under the proposed voting rules, three of the five directors constitute a quorum. Ordinary actions require two affirmative votes with three present, or three affirmative votes with four or five present. Amendments and other matters requiring a larger vote remain subject to their specific rules. Action without a meeting requires every director's written or electronic consent; informal email exchanges or majority-only approvals do not suffice.
